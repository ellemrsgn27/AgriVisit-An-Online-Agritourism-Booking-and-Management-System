More time in the field, less time in the inbox

Local farms have so much to share.

An orchard walk. A first seeding. A meal made with what was grown a few steps away. Tourism can offer small farms an additional source of income, while helping visitors understand the land and the people behind their food.

Welcoming guests shouldn't mean juggling messages.

Social media inquiries and handwritten reservations can leave hosts with double bookings, missed visits and unclear income records. AgriVisit is designed to bring discovery, schedules, limited slots and digital tickets into one organized place.

Our purpose is simple: make authentic farm visits easier to find and manage, and support local agriculture and rural tourism along the way.

For the people who grow

The farm comes first.

Hosts decide what to share, what each experience costs, when to welcome visitors and how many guests they can comfortably accommodate.

A clear booking list and arrival check-in help a working farm prepare. Income and cost records help owners understand which experiences work for them — without replacing the work that makes their farm special.

Become a host

From a little curiosity to a day well spent.

  1. Discover something real

    Search farms on the map by location, experience type, price and rating. Read what your host offers and what to bring.

  2. Make room, not overbookings

    Choose a date and session with available slots. Your host confirms the request before your unique QR e-ticket is issued.

  3. A warm welcome at the gate

    Download your ticket from My bookings. Your host verifies it once on arrival to check your group in.

  4. Leave something useful behind

    After a completed visit, share a rating and review to help the next visitor and give your host thoughtful feedback.

Be a guest of the land

Leave room for the rhythms of a real farm.

Respect the working space
Follow your host's guidance, pick only where invited and keep to marked paths.
Come prepared
Read age and accessibility guidance, arrive for your booked session and bring appropriate footwear.
Keep your host in the loop
If plans change, manage your booking early and check the cancellation terms for your visit.

A thoughtful system for a local purpose.

AgriVisit is an online agritourism booking and management system with an interactive farm map, QR ticketing and a revenue dashboard. Tourist, farm owner and administrator workspaces are designed around their different responsibilities.
